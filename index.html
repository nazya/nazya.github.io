Nazarii Tupitsa

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in Abu Dhabi, where I work with Martin Takáč, Samuel Horváth, and Eduard Gorbunov. I received my PhD from the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT), where I worked in Alexander Gasnikov’s group before joining MBZUAI.

I work on optimization algorithms for machine learning: large-scale, distributed, and federated learning, with robustness and privacy constraints. My current interests include generalized smoothness and adaptive methods.
